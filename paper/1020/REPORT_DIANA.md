# Short report for Diana — 10/20 meeting (Guandao) and this week's results (updated 10/21 13:30 CDT)

**Framing agreed with Guandao (10/20).** An *edge-cloud collaborative world model*: the 14B LingBot-World v2 action/camera-conditioned model is squeezed to fit a 24 GB consumer GPU and runs on the device; the original 14B on the cloud follows the same action stream and corrects the device (KV re-anchoring / drift correction) when needed. Claims to support: better than edge-only (the squeezed model alone), better than the teacher (which does not fit the device), better than any model that fits the device (Waypoint-1.5 / ABot-World / Matrix-Game 2.0 are 20–40 fps but game-only inputs and a small VAE), and a favourable trade-off vs cloud-only.

**Are we first? (literature survey, 10/20, paper/1020/data/lit_edge_cloud_1020.md).** Yes with a precise claim, no with a loose one. No paper splits an *interactive / action-conditioned* video world model between a device and a cloud, or uses a cloud model to re-anchor an on-device autoregressive video generator's history. But edge-cloud collaborative *diffusion* inference exists (image: Hybrid SD 2408.06646, RISE, DiffusionX, MEG; offline video: EcoVideo ECCV 2026 2606.30557, EC-Diff 2507.11980) and edge-cloud *speculative decoding* exists for LLMs (PicoSpec, PipeSD, E2-SCI CVPR 2026, "Speculation at a Distance" 2606.25091) and AR image generation (CIAR 2603.25463). Safe claim: "the first edge-cloud collaborative interactive video world model, and the first to use a cloud model for speculative verification and KV-cache re-anchoring of an on-device autoregressive video generator." MonarchRT (2602.12271) is sparse attention on one GPU, not a split; usable as a device speed-up.

**Why the device is the squeezed 14B and not the 1.3B (main result).** Divergence-injection probe (5 scenes × 10 seeds, 13 chunks, 832×480): when the 1.3B's context is replaced by 14B latents it keeps the injected content but continues with its own dynamics, leaves the 14B seed spread again within ~4 chunks, gains no per-frame quality (judge +0.00), and each re-anchor is a hard cut (2.55× frame jump). Handing generation to the 14B on the 1.3B's context does not help (the 14B inherits the 1.3B's world). The same-model control, now complete on all 5 scenes (14B 2-step ← 14B 4-step): the 2-step device is already inside the 4-step seed spread when alone (0.2–0.6) and one injection pins it to the 4-step mean path (0.01–0.09) for the rest of the clip; follow ratio 0.61–0.74 vs the 1.3B's 0.53–0.64. Cloud correction works inside one model family only.

The 32 GB class is faster than the original (shorter KV window); the 24 GB class pays 0.6–0.7× for the INT4 dequant and is a memory trick only; 10/13 judge: GPTQ-4 −0.58 vs FP8 at 832. Real time (16 fps) is reached at 640×352 by the 32 GB class only. TTFF for the original tiers: 7.6 s (832) / 4.5 s (640); the capped arms have no timed decode in these runs. The 60-s device-only vs +cloud runs are scored (next paragraph).

**60-s degradation x repair matrix (scored 10/21; paper/1020/data/matrix60_1021.md, frames figs/m60/).** Device = 14B 2-step with one squeeze at a time (KV window 12 / 9, FP8 KV, GPTQ-4 weights, the 32 GB and 24 GB bundles); +cloud = the same device stream with the full 14B re-anchoring every chunk; 4 clips x 60 s per cell on the Matrix-Game set (640x352) and the neutral set (832x480). (1) On mg2 every device-only tier, the 4-step teacher included, leaves the scene by 20 s (the hut becomes sky); the cloud-corrected stream keeps a landscape for the full minute, lowers judge drift 81–90 → 58–68, improves camera following 3–4x (RPE rot 0.7–1.2 → 0.21–0.35) and VBench +3–4, at 0.5–0.7x the device fps. (2) At 832 every arm including the teacher replaces the scene within 20 s on these 60-s programs; the cloud improves RPE (0.7–1.0 → 0.46–0.58) and VBench but not the judge's drift score — the 832 drift is the base model's own long-horizon behaviour, not the squeeze. (3) The squeeze factors are within noise of each other on drift / quality with n = 4; the 24 GB class costs speed (12.4 fps at 640, 7.4 at 832), not drift. (4) KV window 9 (sink + one chunk) is the floor and it is unusable (camera control lost, RPE 3.8–4.5, a new unrelated image every few chunks) and unrepairable: the cloud's correction arrives one chunk late and the chunk is already out of the ring (0 of 324 applied) — the smallest repairable window is 12. Needed next: more clips per cell and a scene-persistence metric (the whole-clip judge drift is noise-level between arms at n = 4).

**Guandao's strongest collaboration baseline: cloud low-res + device super-resolution (scored, paper/1020/data/ressplit_port.md).** Cloud 14B 2-step at 576x320, device upsamples to 832x480. Bilinear only: unusable (judge 5.4 vs 7.6, the latent-upsample lattice). With the 1.3B refiner LoRA on the device: judge quality 7.54 vs the full-res 2-step's 7.60 (astra 8.00 vs 7.76), VBench 74.2 vs 74.1, camera RPE rot 0.76 = 0.76, at 12.6 vs 9.6 fps gen (1.3x), 48 GiB serial on one GPU; it loses on translation RPE (0.38 vs 0.29) and TTFF (11.6 s). This is the row the hybrid must beat on the device-fps axis; note it is a cloud-only world model with a device post-processor (content decided at 576 on the cloud, no actions reach the device model), so it does not address the offline / bandwidth / latency arguments for on-device generation.

**Device speed tricks tried this week (training-free, on the stock 14B).** (a) Multi-resolution denoising steps (x3→x2 at half res): 2.1× faster (10 fps at 832, 2-step) but the half-res first step changes the scene content from chunk 1 (judge drift +18, sharpness −20 %) — content is decided at step 1; this needs a schedule-aware fine-tune (Vista). (b) Soft KV re-anchor (device context ← own + α·(cloud − own)): a latent blend is not a transition — α < 0.75 does not anchor, α = 0.5 ghosts (double exposure), α = 1 cuts (1.73× an ordinary boundary, down from the 1.3B's 2.55×); a progressive 0.25→0.5→1 re-anchor keeps every jump ≤ 1.15× at −0.17 judge quality and is the current best. (c) The clean commit pass is 42 % of a 2-step chunk: fused commit (10/11) is the cheaper free lever.

**Status of the frozen baseline matrix.** 10-s sets (Waypoint / MG2 / ABot / Astra / neutral) are frozen with Waypoint-1.5, Matrix-Game 2.0, ABot-World-0, Astra and Speculative Forcing in their own settings (audit 10/19, PROTOCOL.md §8). 60-s clips, the 32 / 24 GB device arms, the degradation × repair matrix and the res-split baseline are in the queue (GPUs 0–3). Judge: Qwen3-VL-32B; VBench / RPE scorers running.
